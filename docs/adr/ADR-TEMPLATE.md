## Context
Describe the problem, constraints, invariants, evidence and why a durable decision is required.

## Decision
State the decision precisely. Identify the new authority boundary and what is explicitly not authoritative.

## Alternatives considered
Record viable alternatives and the reason each was not selected.

## Consequences
Document positive, negative and operational consequences, including new maintenance or cost obligations.

## Compatibility and migration
Explain additive/backward-compatibility rules, immutable historical evidence, upcasters/read compatibility and migration sequencing.

## Security, privacy and rights
Describe trust boundaries, secret/data handling, provenance/rights implications and fail-closed behavior.

## Observability and cost
Define correlation/version evidence, telemetry implications and expected compute/storage/operational cost changes.

## Rollout plan
Define sequencing, feature flags or compatibility windows, ownership and stop conditions.

## Validation and acceptance gates
List deterministic tests, fixtures, benchmarks, runtime/CI gates and exact evidence required before acceptance.

## Evidence
Bind accepted evidence to repository, branch, commit SHA, test/benchmark identifiers and relevant artifacts.

## Rollback / supersession
Explain how to disable, roll back or supersede the decision without rewriting immutable historical evidence.
